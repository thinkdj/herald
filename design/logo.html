Marks for Herald

The tower Herald started with, and the ways it could go. The Gatehouse was chosen and is the mark in the app. Each one is tested at favicon size, as an app icon, and beside the name. Colours follow your system theme.

Wordmark

Lower case, tight, with the l built as a tower. It works with any mark above or alone, and it is the only place the tower needs to appear if the mark moves on.

What shipped

Gatehouse is the mark: header, favicon, about page. It is the only one that is both the letter and the tower, and it is a single path.

Seal could be its formal dress, for large or ceremonial places: the README cover, the sign-in page, a stamp beside a signed release.

The Seal and the tower wordmark are not in the app.
